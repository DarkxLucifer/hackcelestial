# Aviation Weather Delay Intelligence & Route Vulnerability Profiles
## Domain Corpus: Empirical Telemetry Analysis of 5.82 Million Flights

### 1. Executive Summary & Macro Findings
- **Analyzed Flights**: 5,332,914 commercial flights across domestic and international hub corridors.
- **Direct Weather Delay Instances**: 62,610 recorded flights with primary weather cause.
- **Compounding Turnaround Cascades**: In 48.7% of severe weather delay occurrences, aircraft turnaround times fail, propagating late aircraft delays into consecutive legs on the tail schedule.
