SECTION 3: AIRCRAFT TURNAROUND MECHANICS & CASCADING DOMINO PROPAGATION
================================================================================
Turnaround Dynamics:
1. Scheduled Turnaround Time (STT): 40 to 55 minutes for standard narrow-body aircraft (Boeing 737 / Airbus A320).
2. Minimum Viable Turnaround Time (MTT): Strictly 35 minutes for passenger deplaning, cabin sanitization, minimum fueling, and passenger boarding.
3. Turnaround Slack: Scheduled_Turnaround_Time - Minimum_Turnaround_Time (typically 10 to 15 minutes).
4. Domino Propagation Equation:
   Propagated_Late_Aircraft_Delay = max(0, Inbound_Arrival_Delay - (STT - MTT))
5. Cumulative Multi-Leg Ripple Progression:
   - Leg 1 (Morning): Flight encounters 50-minute weather ground delay at ORD. Arrives +45 minutes late at Hub.
   - Gate Turnaround: STT=45m, MTT=35m (10m buffer). Outbound Leg 2 departs +35 minutes late.
   - Leg 2 (Midday): En-route headwinds add 10 minutes. Arrives +45 minutes late.
   - Leg 3 (Afternoon): Inbound delay of 45 minutes against a 40-minute scheduled turn induces +40 minutes late departure.
   - Leg 4 (Evening): Flight arrives +75 minutes late. Flight crew reaches FAA Part 117 / DGCA Flight Duty Time Limitation (FDTL) threshold (maximum 14-hour continuous duty).
   - Mandatory Crew Duty Timeout triggers outright cancellation of subsequent evening return legs, stranding passengers.

SECTION 6: STATUTORY PASSENGER RIGHTS & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK
================================================================================
1. DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV (Directorate General of Civil Aviation, India):
   - Adverse Weather Classification: Meteorological conditions are classified as 'Act of God / Extraordinary Circumstances', exempting airlines from statutory fixed cash compensation.
   - Non-Waivable Duty of Care:
     * Delays >= 2 hours (block time <= 2.5 hours): Free meals and refreshments suited to waiting time.
     * Delays >= 3 hours (block time 2.5 to 5.0 hours): Free meals and refreshments.
     * Delays >= 4 hours (all other flights): Free meals, refreshments, and communication access.
     * Overnight Delays: Free hotel accommodation and airport transfers for delays exceeding 24 hours or overnight delays for flights scheduled between 20:00 and 03:00.
     * Delay > 5 hours: Passenger has the absolute right to cancel the booking and receive a 100% full statutory refund within 7 working days.
2. European Union Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 (EU261 / UK261):
   - Extraordinary Circumstances: Meteorological hazards exempt airlines from Article 7 lump-sum compensation (€250 to €600).
   - Article 9 Mandatory Duty of Care: Free food, beverages, 2 free telephone calls/emails, and hotel accommodation plus transfers if an overnight stay becomes necessary.
   - Article 8 Right to Reimbursement: Delays > 5 hours entitle the traveler to a full refund of the ticket cost within 7 days and a complimentary flight back to the initial departure point if the trip no longer serves its original purpose.
3. United States DOT (14 CFR Part 259 - Enhanced Protections for Airline Passengers):
   - Tarmac Delay Rule: Maximum 3 hours on domestic flights, 4 hours on international flights before carriers must provide passengers an opportunity to safely deplane.
   - Food and Potable Water: Must be provided no later than 2 hours after tarmac delay begins. Operable lavatories and medical assistance must remain available.

# Domino Turnaround Propagation & Multi-Modal Resilience Rules
## Architectural Rules for Autonomous Disruption Management

### 1. Tail Routing Domino Multiplier
Commercial airframes execute 4 to 6 discrete flight legs per calendar day. When Leg 1 sustains a weather delay:
- **Delay Absorption**: Turnaround buffers at hub gates are typically 40-55 minutes. Delays under 30 minutes are partially absorbed.
- **Critical Threshold (>45 minutes)**: When weather delay exceeds 45 minutes, absorption capacity collapses. 100% of remaining delay propagates to Leg 2.
- **Late Aircraft Correlation Factor**: Empirical data confirms that 71.4% of weather delays create compounding late aircraft turnaround delays on subsequent legs.
- **Crew Duty Limitations (FAR Part 117 / DGCA CAR)**: Flights delayed past 90 minutes carry a 34% risk of flight crew duty-time expiration, escalating a delay into a structural overnight cancellation.

### 2. Multi-Modal Inter-Connection Thresholds
When an air journey connects to regional transit (Express Train, Long-Distance Rail, Inter-City Coach, Ferry):
- **Standard Connection Minimum**: 45 minutes for domestic flights; 90 minutes for international customs/baggage.
- **Weather-Compounded Buffer**: If departure airport reports rain >10 mm/h or winds >35 km/h, the required Minimum Connection Time (MCT) must be dynamically expanded by +35 minutes.
- **Autonomous Ghost Hold Trigger**: If projected arrival time exceeds transfer departure time minus MCT, the system must trigger automated provisional holds on next-available high-speed train or luxury coach corridors.

### 3. Hospitality & Hotel Late Check-in Protection
- **Check-in Cutoff Danger Zone**: European and Indian boutique and resort hotels maintain rigid front-desk cutoffs at 21:00 to 23:00 local time.
- **No-Show Cancellation Risk**: If final arrival is delayed past 22:00, automated reservation systems mark guests as 'No Show', releasing rooms and imposing 100% penalty fees.
- **Autonomous Twin Action**: The Voyage Digital Twin must dispatch an authenticated hotel late check-in notification when cumulative itinerary delay breaches 45 minutes, locking the reservation until 03:00 AM.
